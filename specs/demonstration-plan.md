# Demonstration guide specification

Status: planning only. This is a guide structure for the eventual implemented build, not an executable scenario or participant administration. Source: MS-2026-11-09, §3.5 (PDF pp. 71-72) and §3.7.6 (pp. 85-86).

## Required baseline record

Before demonstration, record software/build version, configuration, selected repository identity and exact revisions, artifact inventory, selected language and document formats, dependencies, operating requirements, per-function support matrix and known limitations. The source requires these disclosures in §3.5 p. 71 and §3.7.6 pp. 85-86. Exact values remain unfilled until observed on an implemented release.

The accepted test-project identity is Healthchecks, with Python, Markdown and now plain text in the proposed core. Allowed acquisition, exact commits, eligible inventory and expected outcomes remain pending. No existing local checkout outside the prototype is authorized as an evidence source.

## Common guided tasks

The following four tasks derive from §3.5, PDF p. 72. Concrete inputs and expected outputs must be recorded before they are executed; they are not supplied by the current synthetic frontend.

| Task | Required activity | Planned evidence |
|---|---|---|
| G-01 | Locate a supported artifact and inspect its source and revision. | Selected artifact/version, search or graph path, source availability and support limits. |
| G-02 | Inspect a candidate relationship and evidence; record a review decision and rationale. | Typed assertion, exact endpoints/evidence, authorized actor, reason and retained event. |
| G-03 | Examine a prepared artifact change and inspect resulting review status. | Exact comparison, supported changed path, trigger reason, incoming state and historical confirmation. |
| G-04 | Revalidate the relationship and retrieve its retained prior decision. | Incoming decision, earlier decision/evidence, explicit revision distinction and remaining follow-up. |

Use the same documented starting state and instructions for comparable participant exposure (§3.5, pp. 71-72). Specify an isolated reset procedure and its observable outcome before use. Do not reset a user's working repository or data as part of writing this guide.

## Boundary and extension evidence

Include at least one negative/boundary example such as unsupported evidence or an unrelated change (§3.7.6, p. 85). Record unknown coverage; absence from an incomplete impact set is not proof that an artifact is unaffected (§3.7.5, p. 84).

Working extensions, proposed provisions and unsupported functions must be identified separately (§1.5, pp. 21-23; §3.7.6, p. 86). If an assistant or role-specific extension is implemented, its additional fixed task and exposure are recorded separately (§3.5, p. 72). No such extension is assumed here.

## Readiness versus research administration

Implementation checks link requirement IDs to observed results, failures, limitations and guided tasks (§3.7.6, pp. 85-86). A structurally valid spec is not a passed demonstration.

The manuscript separately requires institutional/ethical clearance before recruitment and administration (§3.5, p. 70), content validation and pilot review (§3.3, p. 69), and a pilot-set common task-time allowance (§3.5, p. 71). Those are research prerequisites, not product features or authorization to collect data now. This guide records them as pending research readiness, without inventing consent, panel attendance or survey results.

Task completion, assistance and deviations are administration context, not comparative productivity evidence (§3.5, p. 72). Practitioner adequacy ratings and developmental checks do not establish independent precision/recall or long-term effectiveness (§1.5 p. 23; §3.7.6 p. 86).
